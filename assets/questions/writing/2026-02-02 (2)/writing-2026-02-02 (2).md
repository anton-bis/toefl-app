## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

Identity: You are a university student living in a dormitory. Recently, you have noticed that the internet connection in your dormitory is very slow and often disconnects. This issue is making it difficult for you to complete your assignments and attend online classes. You want to report this problem to the dormitory manager, Mr. Evans, and request a solution.
To: Mr. Evans
Subject: Internet connection issues in dormitory
Requirements:
- Describe the issue with the internet connection.
- Explain how this problem is affecting your studies.
- Request that the internet connection be improved as soon as possible.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

Subject: psychology
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We've been talking about the role of emotional intelligence in personal and professional settings. Emotional intelligence involves the ability to recognize, understand, and manage our own emotions and the emotions of others. Do you think emotional intelligence is more important than technical skills in the workplace? Why or why not?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Kelly: I think emotional intelligence is more important than technical skills in the workplace. Being able to communicate effectively, manage stress, and work well in teams can lead to better collaboration and a healthier work environment.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Paul: In my opinion, technical skills are more important in the workplace. Without the necessary technical expertise, employees wouldn't be able to perform their tasks efficiently. Emotional intelligence is valuable, but technical skills are essential for job performance.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
