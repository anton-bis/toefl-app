## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Writing Center Feedback
Identity: You recently visited the writing center at your university. You want to write an email to Ms. Garcia, the writing center director, to describe what was helpful, suggest one improvement, and explain why your suggestion would help students.
To: Ms. Garcia
Subject: Feedback on the Writing Center
Requirements:
- Describe what was helpful about your visit.
- Suggest one improvement to the writing center.
- Explain why your suggestion would help students.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

subtitle: Personal Finance Education
Subject: education and life skills
Instructor: Dr. Lee
professor_image: avatar-d-5.png
Professor: Should schools teach personal finance to prepare students for future money management? Explain your opinion clearly.
student_a_image: avatar-d-1.png
Paul: I think schools should teach personal finance because students need to know how to budget and save before they become independent.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Claire: I agree it is useful, but schools already have many required subjects, so I worry that finance lessons might take time away from academic courses.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
