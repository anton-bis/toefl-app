## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Campus Gym Equipment Maintenance
Identity: You recently joined the campus gym and have been enjoying the facilities. However, you have noticed that some of the equipment is not properly maintained. You need to contact the gym manager, Mr. Adams, to report the issue and suggest improvements.
To: Mr. Adams
Subject: Concerns About Equipment
Requirements:
- Mention specifically what you like most about the gym.
- Describe the issues with the equipment and the potential risks.
- Suggest ways to improve the equipment.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

subtitle: Role of Art in Society
Subject: art history
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: Today we'll discuss the dynamic role of art in society. Clearly, art can be a powerful form of expression and communication that can challenge societal norms and help society progress. On the other hand, some people believe that art primarily serves as a way to preserve both the history and heritage of a people, a country, or even a civilization. Which do you believe plays a larger role in the significance of art in society? Why?
student_a_image: avatar-d-4.png
Claire: I think that art can challenge societal norms by presenting alternative perspectives to conventional thinking. For instance, abstract art often provokes critical thinking about established norms and deeply held beliefs. In doing so, it encourages audiences to open their minds to new possibilities and to effect change.
student_b_image: avatar-d-7.png
Paul: I believe that art's role in preserving heritage is its most important role. As the world becomes more globalized, different cultures are losing their identities because they have been forced to adapt to the modern world. The arts have the power to prevent languages from being lost, can preserve important cultural traditions, and remind us of what makes us unique.
Requirements:
- Express and support your personal opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
