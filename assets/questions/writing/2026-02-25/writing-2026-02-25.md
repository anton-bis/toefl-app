## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

Identity: You are a university student living in a dormitory. Recently, you have noticed that the internet connection in your dormitory is very slow and often disconnects. This issue is making it difficult for you to complete your assignments and attend online classes. You want to report this problem to the dormitory manager, Mr. Evans, and request a solution.
To: Mr. Evans
Subject: Internet connection issues in dormitory
Requirements:
- Describe the issue with the internet connection.
- Explain how this problem is affecting your studies.
- Request that the internet connection be improved as soon as possible.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

Subject: economics
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We often discuss the impact of government intervention in the economy. Some argue that government regulations and policies can improve economic stability and protect consumers. Others believe that too much intervention can stifle innovation and lead to inefficiencies. What do you think is the most effective role of government in the economy?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Claire: I think government intervention is important for ensuring economic stability and protecting consumers. Regulations can prevent businesses from engaging in harmful practices and help maintain fair competition. Without government oversight, the economy could become chaotic.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Andrew: In my opinion, too much government intervention can hinder economic growth and innovation. Businesses need the freedom to operate without excessive regulations, which can be costly and create inefficiencies. The economy would benefit more from government with a limited role.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
