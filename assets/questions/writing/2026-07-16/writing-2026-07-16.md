## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Printing Services for a Play
Identity: You are part of your school's drama club and have been assigned the role of event coordinator for an upcoming play. You need to arrange for promotional materials, such as posters and flyers, to be printed and distributed. You want to contact a local print shop to discuss this.
To: Ms. Clark
Subject: Printing Services for Upcoming Play
Requirements:
- Describe the event and its importance.
- Ask about their services for printing promotional materials.
- Inquire about the costs and turnaround time for printing.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

subtitle: Social Media's Influence
Subject: sociology
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We've been exploring how social media platforms influence public opinion in today's connected world. These platforms allow ideas, news, and perspectives to spread quickly, giving individuals and organizations powerful tools to shape conversations. From raising awareness to mobilizing support, social media plays a key role in how people engage with current events. Do you think its influence is mostly beneficial, or should we be more cautious about its reach and impact?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Claire: I think social media has a negative impact on public opinion. It can often create echo chambers, where people only hear opinions that reinforce their existing beliefs. For example, if my friends on social media all seem to be saying the same thing, how can we be challenged to think more independently?
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Andrew: I believe that the influence of social media is mostly beneficial. It allows people to access a wide range of viewpoints and engage in all kinds of discussions and across languages. This enhances understanding and awareness of different issues.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
