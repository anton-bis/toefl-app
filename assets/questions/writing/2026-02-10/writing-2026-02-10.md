## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

Identity: You are a student who recently participated in a group project for your marketing class. One of your group members, Lisa, did an excellent job on part of the project.
To: Lisa
Subject: Appreciation for your excellent contribution
Requirements:
- Thank her for her excellent contribution to the group project.
- Explain what part of Lisa's contribution you appreciated most and how her efforts helped the team succeed.
- Suggest working together on future projects and ask for her opinion.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

Subject: educational psychology
Instructor: Dr. Martinez
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We've been discussing different approaches to student assessment. Traditional exams and standardized tests are commonly used to objectively measure student performance. Some educators, however, advocate for alternative measurements of aptitude other than tests, such as project-based activities and class participation. Which approach do you think is more effective for measuring student learning? Why?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Claire: I believe project-based activities are the most effective way to measure student learning. They allow students to apply their knowledge in real-world contexts and demonstrate their skills through practical tasks. This approach encourages deeper understanding and creativity.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Paul: In my opinion, traditional exams and standardized tests are still the best way to assess student performance. They provide a consistent, objective measure of what students have learned and allow for comparison across different groups of students.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
