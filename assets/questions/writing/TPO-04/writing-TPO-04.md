## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

Identity: You are a student who has been working on a research project and need to request an extension on the deadline from your professor, Dr. Jones. You have encountered some unexpected challenges that have delayed your progress.
To: Dr. Jones
Subject: Request for extension on research project deadline
Requirements:
- Ask for an extension on the deadline.
- Provide details about the challenges you have encountered.
- Suggest a new deadline and offer to provide updates on your progress.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

Subject: anthropology
Instructor: Dr. Achebe
professor_image: avatar-d-4.png
Professor: As we have discussed in class, anthropologists are scientists who usually study cultural rituals and traditions to understand the values and beliefs of different societies. Some believe that anthropologists should participate in these rituals, while others argue that they should observe these rituals from a distance rather than participate in them. What is your perspective on this issue? Why?
student_a_image: avatar-d-5.png
Kelly: I believe that participating in cultural rituals is essential because it helps anthropologists gain deep insights about the community. By actively engaging in these practices, anthropologists can experience firsthand the emotions and significance behind them, leading to a more authentic understanding of the culture.
student_b_image: avatar-d-6.png
Andrew: I think observation from a distance is sufficient and more ethical. Participating in rituals can sometimes be intrusive and disrespectful to the community. Observing allows anthropologists to study cultural practices while minimizing their impact on the society.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
