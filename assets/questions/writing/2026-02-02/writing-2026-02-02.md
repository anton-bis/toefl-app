## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

Identity: You recently joined a new yoga class at your local community center. The instructor, Mr. Davis, has been very welcoming and helpful. However, you find the class schedule inconvenient and would prefer if the classes were held at a different time that fits better with your daily routine.
To: Mr. Davis
Subject: Request for rescheduling yoga classes
Requirements:
- Thank him for his support and guidance in the yoga class.
- Explain why the current class schedule is inconvenient for you.
- Suggest alternative times that would work better for you and possibly other participants.
- Write as much as you can in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

Subject: history
Instructor: Dr. Diaz
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We've been discussing the impact of major historical figures on their respective societies. Some historians argue that individuals like political leaders or influential thinkers shape the course of history more than broader social and economical forces. Others believe social and economical events are the primary drivers of historical change. Do you think the course of history is more shaped by individual actions or broader social and economic forces? Why?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Kelly: I believe individuals and their actions have a greater impact on shaping history. Leaders and influential thinkers can inspire movements, create significant changes, and leave a lasting legacy. Their decisions and actions often alter the course of events in profound ways.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Andrew: In my opinion, broader social and economic events are more significant in shaping history. While individuals can influence events, it is the underlying conditions and trends that drive major changes. These forces affect large groups of people and create the context in which individuals operate.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
