## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Request for Class Notes
Identity: You are a student who missed a recent class. You want to ask your classmate, Jessica, for her notes and any important details from the class.
To: Jessica
Subject: Request for class notes
Requirements:
- Explain why you missed the class.
- Ask for her notes and explain why you need them.
- Suggest what you could do for her in return.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

subtitle: Storytelling in Communication
Subject: communications
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-3.png
Professor: We have been discussing the role of storytelling in effective communication. Storytelling can be a powerful tool to convey messages and connect with audiences emotionally. Some experts argue that storytelling is essential for engaging presentations, while others believe that factual, straightforward communication is more effective. What is your opinion on the use of storytelling in communication?
student_a_image: avatar-d-5.png
Kelly: Storytelling is essential for successful communication during, for instance, presentations. It helps to connect with the audience on an emotional level, making the message more memorable and impactful. Stories can illustrate points more vividly than plain facts and keep the audience engaged from start to finish.
student_b_image: avatar-d-6.png
Paul: Factual, straightforward communication is more effective. Clear and concise information ensures that the audience understands the message without any ambiguity. While stories can be engaging, they may sometimes distract from the main points. Successful communication is determined more by its ability to convey a message than it is to entertain an audience.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
