## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Thanking an Industry Expert
Identity: You recently attended a technology conference where you met Mr. Smith, a prominent industry expert. You found his presentation helpful and informative. You want to thank Mr. Smith for his presentation and request additional information on a topic he discussed.
To: Mr. Smith
Subject: Your Presentation
Requirements:
- Mention what you found useful about his presentation.
- Request additional information about one of the topics he discussed.
- Express your appreciation for his time and insights.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

What approach do you think leads to better long-term business success?
