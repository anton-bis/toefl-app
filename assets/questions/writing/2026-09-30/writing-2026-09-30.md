## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Internship: Additional Information
Identity: You are a student who recently applied for a summer internship at a company. You have received an email from the personnel manager, Mr. Davis, asking you to provide additional documents and information to complete your application.
To: Mr. Davis
Subject: Summer internship application - additional documents
Requirements:
- Thank him for considering your application.
- Explain what interests you most about the job for which you are applying.
- List the additional documents and information you are sending.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.
