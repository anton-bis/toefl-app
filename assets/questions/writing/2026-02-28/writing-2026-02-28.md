## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

Identity: Your professor, Dr. Smith, recently assigned a group project due in two weeks. You are frustrated because not all of your group members are contributing equally to the project.
To: Dr. Smith
Subject: Issues with group project
Requirements:
- Describe the issue you are facing with your group members.
- Describe your specific contribution to the project.
- Explain why you and the group have been unable to address this issue.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

Subject: anthropology
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We've been discussing the impact of cultural globalization on local traditions. Some anthropologists argue that cultural globalization leads to the loss of unique and diverse cultural identities, while others believe it promotes cultural exchange and understanding. What is your perspective on the impact of cultural globalization on local traditions?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Kelly: I believe cultural globalization can lead to the loss of unique cultural identities. As global culture becomes more dominant, traditional practices and languages can be forgotten or replaced. It's important to preserve and celebrate local cultures to maintain diversity.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Andrew: In my opinion, cultural globalization promotes cultural exchange and understanding. It allows people to learn about and appreciate different cultures. This exchange can enrich local traditions and create a more interconnected and tolerant world.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
