## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Furniture Purchase Issue
Identity: You recently purchased new furniture for your dorm room from an online store, and you are satisfied with it in general. However, you noticed some issues with the furniture. You need to contact customer service about the issue.
To: Customer Service
Subject: Recent Furniture Purchase
Requirements:
- Mention what you particularly liked about the furniture.
- Describe the issue you noticed with the furniture.
- Suggest a resolution for the problem.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

subtitle: Home Environment vs School Resources
Subject: education
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-4.png
Professor: Today we'll discuss how different factors affect educational outcomes. Clearly, the quality of teaching and the resources available to students play a significant role in their academic success. On the other hand, some people believe that a student's home environment and parental involvement are equally, if not more, important. Which do you believe plays a larger role in a student's educational outcomes? Why?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Claire: I think that the home environment and parental involvement are crucial. Students who have supportive parents and a stable home life tend to perform better academically. Parents who are involved in their child's education can provide additional support and encouragement.
student_b_image: avatar-d-6.png
Paul: I believe that the quality of teaching and the resources available at school are more important. Schools that provide a positive learning environment and the necessary tools for learning can significantly impact a student's academic achievements.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
