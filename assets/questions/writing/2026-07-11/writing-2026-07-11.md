## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Sunny Café Pastry Suggestions
Identity: You recently visited a new café that opened near your campus called Sunny Café. Although you generally enjoyed the café, you were disappointed with some issues.
To: Ms. Garcia
Subject: Suggestions for expanding pastry selection
Requirements:
- Describe what you liked about your visit to the café.
- Explain why you were disappointed with the limited selection of pastries.
- Suggest a few types of pastries that could be added to the menu to improve the selection.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## Write for an Academic Discussion

### Write for an Academic Discussion – 1

subtitle: Brand Loyalty or New Customers
Subject: marketing
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: Next week, we will be discussing the impact of brand loyalty on consumer behavior. Brand loyalty refers to consumers consistently purchasing products from the same brand due to positive experiences and trust. Some marketers argue that building brand loyalty is essential for long-term success, while others believe that attracting new customers should be the priority. What do you think is more important for a company, building brand loyalty or attracting new customers?
student_a_image: avatar-d-2.png
Claire: Building brand loyalty is more important for long-term success. Loyal customers provide consistent revenue and often become brand advocates, attracting new customers through word-of-mouth recommendations.
student_b_image: avatar-d-3.png
Paul: Attracting new customers should be the priority. Expanding the customer base can lead to increased market share and growth opportunities. Focusing on new customers can also diversify the revenue streams for the company.
Requirements:
- Express and support your opinion.
- Make a contribution to the discussion in your own words.
- An effective response will contain at least 100 words.
Hint: You will have 10 minutes to write.
