## Write an Email

### Write an Email – 1

subtitle: Thank-You to the Seminar Coordinator
Identity: You recently attended a seminar focused on communication skills. The seminar was highly valuable to you, and you would like to express your appreciation to the organizer.
To: Seminar Coordinator
Subject: Thank You for the Communication Skills Seminar
Requirements:
- Explain how the seminar was helpful to you.
- Request additional resources for improving communication skills.
- Express your admiration for their expertise in the field.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

### Write an Email – 2

subtitle: Class Trip Agency Recommendation
Identity: Your teacher is planning a class field trip for your class and has asked students to suggest a suitable travel agency or organization to help arrange the trip. You have found an agency that you believe would be a good choice.
To: Teacher
Subject: Recommendation for a Class Trip Agency
Requirements:
- State the purpose of your email.
- Recommend a suitable agency or a specific option.
- Explain why this agency is appropriate for the class field trip.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

### Write an Email – 3

subtitle: Vacation Accommodation Inquiry
Identity: You are going to take a trip and plan to book vacation accommodation. You want to write an email to a travel agency staff member to consult relevant booking information.
To: Travel Agency Staff
Subject: Inquiry About Vacation Accommodation
Requirements:
- State your intention to reserve vacation lodging and clarify your practical accommodation needs.
- Ask the staff to recommend suitable private houses or hotels that fit your requirements.
- Inquire about the price and unique advantages of different options, and expect to receive a best accommodation suggestion.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

### Write an Email – 4

subtitle: Hotel Stay Feedback
Identity: You recently stayed at a hotel during a campus visit. While you generally liked the room, there were some issues with the room during your stay. You need to provide feedback to the hotel manager, Ms. Thompson, and suggest improvements.
To: Ms. Thompson
Subject: Feedback on My Hotel Stay
Requirements:
- Describe what you enjoyed about your stay.
- Explain the issues you had with the room and stay.
- Suggest ways that the hotel could make a guest's stay more peaceful.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

### Write an Email – 5

subtitle: Class Field Trip Travel Agency Inquiry
Identity: You are a student leader at your school and have been assigned to help plan your class field trip. You have found a travel agency that offers comprehensive travel packages and want to contact the travel agent, Mr. Brown, to inquire about their services.
To: Mr. Brown
Subject: Inquiry About Class Field Trip Packages
Requirements:
- Explain the purpose and dates of the class field trip.
- Ask about the available offers for large groups.
- Request details on how they can accommodate specific travel needs and additional services offered.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

### Write an Email – 6

subtitle: Technology Conference Inquiry
Identity: You have signed up to take part in an upcoming technology-themed conference. You want to write an email to the conference organizer to consult relevant matters.
To: Conference Organizer
Subject: Questions About the Technology Conference
Requirements:
- Express your excitement about attending the event and talk about the technology topics you are most interested in.
- Ask about detailed entry procedures and important notes for participants.
- Inquire whether digital documents or relevant materials of the conference can be provided in advance.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

### Write an Email – 7

subtitle: Career Seminar Follow-Up
Identity: You have attended a career planning seminar and regarded its content highly valuable. You wish to stay connected and have further communication with the speaker.
To: Seminar Speaker
Subject: Follow-Up After the Career Planning Seminar
Requirements:
- Point out which seminar topics have benefited you greatly.
- Ask the speaker for access to more related communication and learning resources.
- Express sincere appreciation for the meaningful and insightful content shared in the seminar.
- Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

subtitle: Customer Feedback in Product Development
Subject: product development
Instructor: Dr. Gupta
professor_image: avatar-d-1.png
Professor: We've been discussing the importance of customer feedback in product development. Gathering feedback can help companies understand customer needs and improve their products. However, some argue that relying too heavily on customer feedback can stifle innovation and lead to mediocre products. What is your opinion on this?
student_a_image: avatar-d-3.png
Paul: Customer feedback is essential for product development. It helps companies understand customer needs and preference ensuring that products are tailored to the market. Feedback can guide improvements and enhance customer satisfaction.
student_b_image: avatar-d-5.png
Kelly: I believe that relying too heavily on customer feedback can stifle innovation. Companies should balance feedback with creative vision to avoid producing mediocre products. Innovation often requires taking risks and pursuing new ideas that customers may not initially recognize.
